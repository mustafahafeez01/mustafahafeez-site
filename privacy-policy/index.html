How information is used when you visit mustafahafeez.com.

Who is responsible

I’m Mustafa Hafeez, based in the United Arab Emirates. I run this personal writing and professional-profile website and am responsible for the personal information I process through it. For privacy questions or requests, contact mustafa@mustafahafeez.com.

This notice applies to mustafahafeez.com. Linked products and services publish their own privacy information.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub receives the technical information needed to serve pages and documents that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting activity occurs when you access the site, including when you decline optional analytics.

GitHub’s privacy statement explains its processing, retention and international transfers.

Optional Google Analytics

With your consent, I use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are read and which external links visitors follow. Google receives information associated with those requests, including cookie identifiers, page and referrer URLs, and browser and device information. It can derive an approximate location from the connection.

Page-view events include the full page URL and referrer so campaign attribution can work. Custom link events include the source page, page section and, for external links, the destination hostname. Contact events omit the contact address. Keep sensitive personal information out of URLs when linking to this site.

The site’s configuration disables Google signals and advertising personalisation. Optional analytics loads after you choose Allow analytics on this site’s production HTTPS domain. Declining leaves optional analytics disabled.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s privacy policy.

Cookies and your stored choice

Your browser stores your analytics choice in local storage under mh-analytics-consent. This remembers whether you allowed or declined analytics. It remains until you change it, clear the site’s browser storage or the browser removes it.

After consent, Google Analytics can set first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_<container-id> to recognise browsers and session activity. Google documents a default duration of two years. Browser restrictions and configured settings can shorten this period, and return visits can refresh expiry.

Change or withdraw consent

Open Cookie settings in the footer to review or withdraw your choice. Withdrawal stops subsequent measurement by this site and attempts to remove its accessible _ga cookies. You can also clear this site’s cookies and storage through your browser.

Withdrawal affects future processing. Information already sent remains subject to the applicable retention and deletion processes.

How long information is kept

The Google Analytics property is configured to retain event data for two months and user data for fourteen months. Reset on new user activity is enabled, so new activity can restart the retention period for the user identifier.

Google’s retention controls apply to user-level and event-level data used in explorations and funnel reports. Standard aggregated reports can remain available for longer. Cookie expiry is a separate setting. Google explains its retention controls.

I keep privacy correspondence for the time needed to respond, handle follow-up and meet applicable legal obligations. GitHub and Google describe retention for data they process in their own privacy information.

Providers and international processing

GitHub hosts the site and Google provides optional analytics. They may process information in countries outside your own. Their privacy information describes international transfers and the safeguards they use.

External links take you to services governed by their own policies. If you contact me through email or LinkedIn, the chosen communication provider also processes your message.

Your choices and rights

Optional analytics relies on your consent. I use privacy enquiries to respond to your request, and hosting providers process technical information to deliver and secure their services.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, and complain to the relevant data-protection authority.

Email mustafa@mustafahafeez.com so I can consider your request. Analytics identifiers may be needed to locate a relevant record. Please avoid sending sensitive documents with an initial enquiry.

Updates to this notice

The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes to data handling will be reflected here and, where appropriate, in the consent information.
